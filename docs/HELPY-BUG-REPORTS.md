# Bug reports in Helpy

[Open RuneSchema's Nexus Bugs page](https://www.nexusmods.com/runescapedragonwilds/mods/570?tab=bugs){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

Sign in to a Nexus account and describe:

- What happened and what you expected.
- Steps to reproduce it.
- Your RuneSchema/game versions and platform.
- Relevant diagnostic codes or a short log excerpt, when available.

In the 0.8.1.0 prerelease, **Report a bug** opens this page directly. Older Helpy
builds may show a form or GitHub link; use the Nexus link above instead.
Review logs before sharing them; saves and private account data are not needed
for a basic report.
