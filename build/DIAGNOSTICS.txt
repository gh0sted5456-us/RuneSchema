RuneSchema builder diagnostics

The root launcher remains open on failure and writes:
    build\logs\launcher-latest.log

The launcher starts logging before build.ps1 is parsed, so PowerShell startup,
parse, dependency, CMake, Ninja, packaging, and normal build failures are visible.

Clean builder behavior:
- PowerShell 5.1-compatible configuration fingerprinting.
- CMake automatic regeneration is disabled.
- build.ps1 owns reconfiguration through its CMake/source fingerprint.
- Normal builds skip internal contract tests.
- Use Build RuneSchema.bat -Tests for the supported local contract suite.
- runtime-template contents are assembled into the RuneSchema payload explicitly.
