Helpy backgrounds

Drop your own PNG, JPG/JPEG or BMP pictures into this folder, then open
Settings > Backgrounds > Refresh pictures. Select a picture to see its preview.
Preview in Helpy is temporary. Apply background saves your selection and
image strength. Pictures use an opaque backing behind Helpy's dark tint, so
other windows cannot show through. Select Clear glass (RuneSchema default)
and Apply background to restore the configured transparent window style.

Use 2560x1440 or 3840x2160 for detailed desktop backgrounds. Pixel dimensions
matter, not the file's DPI metadata. Images are fitted proportionally and
cropped rather than stretched. PNG preserves sharp edges and transparency;
JPG is suitable for photographic or painted backgrounds. SVG is not supported.

Limits: 128 pictures, 20 MiB per file, up to 4096 pixels per dimension.
Files remain local and are not uploaded. Symbolic links are not followed.
Your own files are not removed by refreshing or changing the selection.
